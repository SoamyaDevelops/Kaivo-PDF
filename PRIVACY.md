# Privacy Policy for Kaivo PDF

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Kaivo ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the **Kaivo PDF** desktop application (the "App").

---

## 1. Local-First Architecture

Kaivo PDF is designed as a **local-first** application. 
- **Local Processing**: All core operations—including viewing, editing, converting (e.g. converting images to PDF, splitting/merging PDFs), and managing your PDF documents—are performed entirely on your local machine.
- **No Cloud Uploads**: Your files and documents are **never** uploaded to, stored on, or processed by our servers, nor do we share them with any third parties.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute data minimization. Therefore, the App:
- **No Telemetry or Analytics**: Does not run any tracking software, telemetry, or diagnostic reporting that sends data to our servers.
- **No Personal Information**: Does not require user registration, account creation, or input of personal identifiers (such as name, email, or IP address).
- **No Document Tracking**: Does not track the titles, contents, or metadata of the files you open, edit, or convert.

## 3. Local Storage and Preferences

To enhance your user experience, the App stores certain configuration files locally on your device (e.g., in standard system application directories):
- **Recent Files List**: Saved locally to display a list of recently opened files in your sidebar.
- **User Settings**: Remembers options such as Dark Mode preference and Default Reader settings.
- **Local Library**: Tracks reference paths to documents you have saved or edited using Kaivo PDF for quick access.

This data remains strictly on your device and can be cleared at any time by resetting the App or uninstalling it.

## 4. Dependencies and Network Access

Kaivo PDF is built using the Electron framework and relies on trusted, open-source libraries (e.g., `pdf-lib`, `pdfkit`, `mammoth`, `canvas`, and `PDF.js`). 
- These libraries run locally inside the application runtime.
- The App does not initiate background network connections to fetch external document-related assets, keeping your offline environment completely isolated and secure.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted by updating the `PRIVACY.md` file in the official repository. We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any updates.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our privacy practices, please feel free to open an issue in our official [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Kaivo/kaivo-pdf).
